Privacy Policy

REPORT-BOT is an internal tool used by authorized users to collect reports, messages, and files from designated LINE groups.

Collected data may include message content, images, documents, file metadata, timestamps, and related technical information required for system operation.

Data is stored in PostgreSQL and Google Drive for internal operational use, record keeping, and future reporting or dashboard purposes.

REPORT-BOT does not sell collected data and does not use the collected data for advertising purposes.

Access to stored data is limited to authorized users and administrators.

Google account authorization is used only to allow REPORT-BOT to store authorized files in Google Drive.

Users may contact the system administrator regarding access, correction, or deletion requests where applicable.
